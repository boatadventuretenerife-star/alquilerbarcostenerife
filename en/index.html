BEFORE YOU BOARD

The essentials, clear.

Can I book without a skipper?

No. Every trip on this website includes a professional skipper.

Where do we depart from?

The usual boarding point is Marina del Sur in Las Galletas (Arona). You receive full details once confirmed.

What happens in bad weather?

If weather conditions prevent a safe trip, a new date or full refund will be offered.

How does online payment work?

Once availability is confirmed, your booking is completed through Stripe’s secure checkout. We do not store your card details.
